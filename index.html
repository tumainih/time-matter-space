About the mapping

Spirit marks fall every 3 hours (3,6,9,12,15,18,21h) — the start of each quaday. Soul follows, then Body, before the next mark. Wednesday is the week's peak; the fasting month is the peak of each quarter; weeks 4–5 are the peak of the quarter's two working months; year 4 is the peak of each 7‑year cycle.
